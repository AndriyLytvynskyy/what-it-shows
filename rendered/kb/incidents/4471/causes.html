The three problems

  • parser (fixed, fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales): the shared parser read every date month first. Our tenants all write the day first, so day and month swapped: 7 April came out as 4 July. This is what #4471 described.
  • hint (won’t fix, form retired): the booking-v3 form printed MM/DD/YYYY under its date field but read the date day first. People typed what the hint said and the date swapped. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants. The parser fix did not touch the form; retiring it did.
  • shift (open): the booking moves by one day, no swap; the same meeting shows Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto. A timezone or day-boundary bug. The desk noted these (desk-notes.json, oneDayMove) and filed them under parser by tenant, so they went out with #4471. The parser fix does not change them.

How it was counted

  • One id, one report: the export pages overlap, so some reports come twice.
  • Cause by tenant locale, booking form and channel (causes.ts), not by intake tag, the same view as #4471’s table. Intake guesses from the browser; the table shows how often it guessed a different cause.
  • The shift reports are the desk’s own list, checked against every report body: the others quote a day/month swap or no comparable dates.

For the desk

  • A one-day move is not a swap. Send it to the shift ticket, not to parser.
  • Before merging into a ticket, check that the report has the same cause, not the same subject. The dedupe explorer already shows the difference.
