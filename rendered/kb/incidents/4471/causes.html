The three problems

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Our tenants all write the day first, so day and month swap: 7 April comes out as 4 July. This is what #4471 describes.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field but reads the date day first. People type what the hint says and the date swaps. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants. The parser fix does not touch the form.
  • shift: the booking moves by one day, no swap; the same meeting shows Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto. A timezone or day-boundary bug. The desk already noted these (desk-notes.json, oneDayMove) and filed them under parser by tenant.

How it was counted

  • One id, one report: the export pages overlap, so some reports come twice.
  • Cause by tenant locale, booking form and channel (causes.ts), not by intake tag. Intake guesses from the browser; the table shows how often it guessed a different cause.
  • The shift reports are the desk’s own list, checked against every report body: the others quote a day/month swap or no comparable dates.

For the desk

  • Before merging into a ticket, check that the report has the same cause, not the same subject. The dedupe explorer already shows the difference.
  • Reports from booking-v3 forms go to the hint ticket, whatever intake tagged them.
